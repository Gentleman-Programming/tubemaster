# TubeMaster Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-21_

TubeMaster is open-source, self-hosted software. Each person runs their own copy on their own computer. There is no TubeMaster server, account system or company that receives your data.

## What the app handles

When you connect accounts, your copy of TubeMaster stores on your own computer:

- OAuth tokens for Google (YouTube), TikTok and Instagram, so it can act on your behalf. TikTok and Instagram tokens are encrypted at rest.
- Basic profile data returned by those platforms (account id and display name).
- The videos you choose to publish, their captions, options and scheduled times.

## Where data goes

Data leaves your computer only when you ask the app to do something, and only to the platform involved:

- YouTube / Google APIs, to read your channel and manage your videos.
- TikTok APIs, to publish videos you confirmed to your TikTok account.
- Instagram (Meta) APIs, to publish videos you confirmed to your Instagram professional account.

TubeMaster does not sell, share or send your data to anyone else, and it has no analytics or tracking.

## Your control

- Nothing is published without your explicit preview and confirmation.
- You can disconnect an account at any time from the app, and revoke access from each platform's settings.
- Deleting your local copy of TubeMaster (including its `data/` folder) deletes everything it stored.
